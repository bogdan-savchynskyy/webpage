The course consists of lectures and practical exercises and concentrates on optimization techniques for machine learning tasks, such as inference and learning for graphical models. The course contains 3 parts: first, we briefly review basic convex optimization techniques widely used in machine learning. The second part  is devoted to inference problem for graphical models: we consider a number of existing algorithms, show their connection and their analysis from optimization point of view. In the final third partwe consider the parameter's learning of graphical models, both probabilistic and discriminative.
